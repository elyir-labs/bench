# Elyir Bench Company Contributor License Agreement

Version 1.0, DRAFT. Not yet in use: it is being reviewed by a lawyer. Adapted from the Apache Software
Foundation's Software Grant and Corporate Contributor License Agreement (r190612).

**In short.** For a company whose employees contribute to Elyir Bench as part of their work. The company
keeps the copyright, and lets Elyir LLC use, change and pass on the contributions under the GPL and
under other licenses too, including commercial ones. It names the people allowed to contribute for it.
Each of them also agrees to the individual agreement ([CLA.md](CLA.md)) in their first pull request.

**How to sign.** Fill in the details below, sign, and email a PDF to hello@elyir.dev. Tell us when the
list of people in Schedule A changes.

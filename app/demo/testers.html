Got your invite?

Three steps, about ten minutes.

  1. On the printer, over SSH, while it isn't printing

    From a computer. The command is in your email too. It checks the printer first and asks once before it changes anything.

    $curl -fsSL https://bench.elyir.com/install | sh

  2. Scan the code it shows

    With your phone. Your printer opens, ready to go.

  3. Turn on Bench Cloud with your code

    On that phone, open printers.elyir.app/you, sign in with your email and type the code from your invite. Then, on your home Wi-Fi, add your printer there.

Will it work on yours? The board table is on the install page. Changed your mind? elyir-bench remove takes it off and puts KlipperScreen back.

Tell us something

  • On the printer: Care, then "Tell Elyir something". For a problem it adds the printer's logs and settings, with passwords and keys taken out, and shows you what goes first.
  • From any browser: bench.elyir.com/write
  • Or reply to your invite email, or ask in the Elyir Bench Discord.

If the install itself fails, it offers to send us what went wrong.

# Reporting a security problem

Please don't open a public issue for a security problem. Email **security@elyir.dev** instead, with what
you found, how to reproduce it, and which version or commit you were using.

You'll get a reply within a week. Once a fix is out, you'll be credited in its release notes unless you'd
rather not be.

Elyir Bench runs on printers in people's homes, and reaches them from away through the door (`door/`) and a
relay. Problems there matter most: anything that lets someone control, watch or reach a printer that isn't
theirs.
